Povezava z Googlom

Uporabnik lahko LYRA poveže s svojim Google računom (Koledar, Tasks, YouTube, Gmail samo za branje). Dostop odobri sam na Googlovem zaslonu za soglasje in ga lahko kadar koli prekliče. Kako LYRA ravna s temi podatki, opisuje politika zasebnosti.

Personal terminal – everything in one place

LYRA is a private application that runs on the user's own device (home computer or mini PC). There is no shared cloud server: data stays with the user.

Modules include a day planner (Google Calendar and Google Tasks), important mail overview (read-only, several accounts), music (YouTube Music and YouTube), markets and smart home. Users may connect their own Google account; access is granted on Google's consent screen and can be revoked at any time. See the privacy policy.
